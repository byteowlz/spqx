# Voice Studio concept round

Development decision record; not a production target. Owning issue spqx-1we0. Scope: GPUI desktop, macOS/Linux/Windows, personal-first, code-first exploration, no excessive menus. Studio is the visual authority. No page implementation begins before the direction choice. Plain-text concept confirmation uses the structured question tool because serve-question reports a headless environment; no extra review port is started.

## Grounded candidates, ordered before evaluation

1. Casting contact sheet: voices as a compact grid of authored orb identities, selected voice beside the text and direct transport. Strong voice management; risk of browsing dominating creation.
2. Field-recorder control strip: text takes the main region; one orb and transport line anchor the bottom. A very compact read-aloud cousin; less room for a growing library.
3. Library listening booth: a narrow voice index, readable manuscript and fixed transport. Familiar and effective, but risks a generic sidebar/editor arrangement.
4. Cue-card cart: short pieces of text and their saved takes share a compact list. Strong repeat clips; long-form reading becomes secondary.
5. Dubbing deck: explicit source/audition positions and a single transport. Strong comparisons; risk of implying a DAW or simultaneous mixing.
6. Voice specimen desk: one selected orb and a few direct voice choices frame a useful text sample; the transport is at hand. Lab reveals paired auditions in the same footprint, without opening a menu tree. Keep specimen scale compact rather than a giant hero.
7. Reading folio: text leads, voices form an inline margin index, compact player peels off. Strong selected-text use; less discoverable voice creation.

Seed bad676f5 assigned candidate 6. This assignment is not user approval. Category ruts to avoid: an oversized generic dashboard or a single giant glowing orb with hidden functions. Both lose the everyday local-tool job.

## Challenger evaluation

Evaluate audience identification / product clarity separately, before borrowing.

- Cassette j-card → paired take cards: competitive on familiarity of named audio pieces; weaker than specimen desk for arbitrary text creation. Full alternate: compact text sheet, blue/ink hierarchy from canonical theme roles, two explicitly auditioned takes and a timed bottom transport. No cassette decoration, fake durations or automatic A/B synthesis. Carries bounded output identity and clear side labels.
- Zoo guide map: declined / declined. Large organic colored regions obscure a compact text/audio task. Raise retained: an unambiguous single active region; do not borrow its lobed forms or palette.
- Star atlas: competitive on orb identity / declined on task clarity. Do not imply voice similarity or PCA meaning from decorative spatial positions. Keep only stable identity and a clearly marked selection, never a fake embedding map.
- Acetate manual: declined / declined. Many section tabs and material layers conflict with no excessive menus. Raise retained: every frequent control must be findable without opening a hierarchy.
- Streaming catalog wall: competitive on voice browsing / declined on product clarity. Browsing must not hide the text task. Raise retained: selected voice metadata is readable at the point of action, without hover-only disclosure.
- Variable-font specimen: competitive on Lab focus / weaker everyday creation clarity. Continuous visual coordinates cannot imply continuous audio changes. Available as a later Lab refinement, not a replacement for the exact-preview contract. Raise retained: same text, explicit changed/unpreviewed state, pinned comparison.

## Proposed decision set

- Voice specimen desk (assigned): selected orb, compact voice choices, text-first creation, inline transport. Advanced Lab changes the working area, not the navigation hierarchy.
- Casting contact sheet (top-ranked alternative): more voices immediately visible; creation remains beside the selected voice. Honest risk: the familiar library/editor arrangement is less distinctive and consumes more width.
- Paired take cards (competitive challenger): named text/take sheets with explicit A/B positions. Honest risk: excellent short-clip comparison, weaker for long articles and larger voice collections.

The choice approves an exploratory direction only, not a final rendered visual target, production styling, engine capabilities, permission grants or deployment. Actual synthetic Studio mockups and native platform evidence follow separately.
